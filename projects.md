# Projects

Below is a selection of projects that reflect my work in econometrics, impact evaluation, financial inclusion, and digital development.

---

## 📊 1. Causal Effect of Digital Connectivity on Financial Inclusion

**Focus:** Developing a Digital Connectivity Index (DCI) using the FINDEX 2025 database and estimating its causal impact on individual-level financial inclusion outcomes.

**Methods:** Instrumental variables, causal inference techniques, and robustness checks using cross-country microdata.

**Status:** Ongoing

**Highlights:**
- Constructed a Digital Connectivity Index (DCI) from FINDEX 2025 indicators  
- Estimated causal effects on account ownership, mobile money use, and digital payment adoption  
- Designed reproducible workflows for transparent and replicable analysis

## 📚 2. Topic Modeling on ESG Investment and Greenwashing Discourse

**Focus:** Applying BERTopic to map thematic structures in ESG investment literature and identify patterns of greenwashing-related discourse.

**Methods:** BERTopic, transformer-based embeddings, dimensionality reduction, and topic coherence evaluation.

**Status:** Ongoing

**Highlights:**
- Built a curated corpus of ESG and sustainability‑related academic publications  
- Applied BERTopic to extract dominant themes and emerging discourse clusters  
- Identified topic groups related to greenwashing, ESG rating divergence, and sustainability reporting  
- Developed reproducible pipelines for preprocessing, modeling, and visualization

---

## 📖 3. Systematic Literature Review on ESG Investment as Real Options

**Focus:** Conducting a systematic literature review on ESG investment framed as real options, and using BERTopic to thematize conceptual, methodological, and empirical patterns in the existing body of research.

**Methods:** PRISMA-based screening, structured coding, BERTopic for thematic extraction, topic coherence evaluation, and cross-theme synthesis.

**Status:** Ongoing

**Highlights:**
- Developed a structured search and screening protocol for ESG–real options literature  
- Applied BERTopic to identify dominant themes, conceptual clusters, and emerging debates  
- Mapped discourse on investment timing, uncertainty, sustainability commitments, and greenwashing-related strategic behavior
- Built a reproducible workflow integrating text preprocessing, topic modeling, and visualization
  
---

## 🌱 4. Systematic Literature Review on ESG, Green Innovation, and Firms’ Resilience to Economic Shocks

**Focus:** Conducting a systematic literature review to synthesize evidence on how ESG practices and green innovation contribute to firms’ resilience during economic shocks, disruptions, and periods of heightened uncertainty.

**Methods:** PRISMA-based screening, structured coding, thematic synthesis, and BERTopic for automated topic discovery and discourse mapping.

**Status:** Ongoing

**Highlights:**
- Developed a comprehensive search and screening protocol covering ESG, green innovation, and resilience literature  
- Applied BERTopic to identify thematic clusters related to sustainability strategies, innovation pathways, and shock‑absorption mechanisms  
- Mapped conceptual linkages between ESG performance, adaptive capacity, and firm‑level resilience  
- Built a reproducible workflow integrating text preprocessing, topic modeling, and cross-theme synthesis
